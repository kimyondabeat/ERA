ERA - RABODAY DRUMS  v1.0
by kimyondabeat
=========================

INSTALL (Windows, VST3)
1. Run ERA_v1.0_Setup.exe (or copy the "ERA.vst3" folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3).
2. In your DAW, rescan your plugins, then load ERA as an instrument.
ERA works in any DAW that supports VST3 instruments.
To try it without a DAW, open the ERA app.
(If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".)

PADS  (MIDI notes)
KICK C1 (36)   SNARE D1 (38)   RIM C#1 (37)   PERCS G#1 (44)
VOX  A1 (45)   FX    B1 (47)   CLAP D#1 (39)  TOM   F1 (41)

HOME
- Click a pad to play it and select it.
- < > on the screen change the sound of the selected pad.
- CATEGORIES + SAMPLE LIBRARY: click a sound to hear it, star your favorites,
  search by name, double-click or ASSIGN to put it on its pad.
- PAD CONTROL: Attack, Decay, Tune, Volume.
- SAMPLE EDIT: drag S (start) and E (end) on the waveform to cut the sound,
  Fade In, Fade Out, Offset (plays the pad a little later), Stretch (longer or
  shorter, same pitch), Reverse, Normalize, Snap (cuts without clicks), Reset.
- LAYER: stacks a second sound (B) on the pad. Click A or B to edit each one,
  BLEND mixes them. In the library, ASSIGN TO LAYER B picks the B sound.

MIX
- Pan, volume, mute and solo for every pad, with level meters.
- MASTER: Width, Limit (louder, never clips) and the master volume.

FX
- Pick a pad (or MASTER), then use Delay (synced to your DAW tempo), Filter,
  Equalizer, Reverb, Compressor and Transient. Each module has ON / OFF.
- RESET FX puts the selected pad's effects back to default.

SETTINGS
- Interface size (60% to 120%).
- Interface colour: Lime, Ice Blue (black & white), Yellow (black & grey), Pink (grey & white).
